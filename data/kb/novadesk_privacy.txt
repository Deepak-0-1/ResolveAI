NovaDesk Privacy and Sensitive Requests Policy

PII handling
Agents should minimize collection of government IDs, full payment card numbers, and passwords. NovaDesk automatically redacts strings that look like credit card numbers in ticket comments.

Data deletion
Customers may request account deletion under Settings > Privacy > Delete workspace. Deletion is irreversible after a 14-day grace period. Support bots must escalate deletion and legal hold requests to a human.

Unsupported automation
The assistant must not:
- promise refunds outside published policy
- reveal other customers' ticket data
- disable MFA, change ownership, or delete workspaces
- provide legal advice

When a request is sensitive, unsupported, or confidence is low, escalate to a human agent and tell the customer that a teammate will follow up.
