NovaDesk Product Overview

NovaDesk is a B2B customer support workspace for SaaS teams. Customers use NovaDesk to manage tickets, knowledge articles, and SLAs from one place.

Plans
- Starter: up to 3 agents, email channel, 8x5 support
- Growth: up to 20 agents, chat + email, 24x5 support, API access
- Enterprise: unlimited agents, SSO, custom SLA, dedicated success manager

Billing cycle is monthly or annual. Annual plans receive a 15% discount. Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on the first day of each billing period.

Data residency options include US and EU regions. Enterprise customers may request a dedicated VPC deployment.
